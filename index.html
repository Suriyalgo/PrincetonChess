Who We Are

Our mission

We believe everyone should know how to play chess, and have access to over-the-board games. Our club exists to give every Princeton student, beginner or master, a welcoming place to play, learn, and belong. We meet every Friday to push pawns, trade blitz games, teach newcomers, and represent Princeton at tournaments across the country.

This organization is open to all Princeton University students interested in supporting our mission, regardless of identity, such as race, sex, ethnicity, national origin, or other protected characteristics.
