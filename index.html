About Us

More than just a game

We're a community of students, faculty, and staff who love chess — and we'd love for you to be part of it.

♙

All Levels Welcome

Never played, or pushing 2000? We pair beginners with experienced members and run lessons throughout the semester.

♛

Compete & Improve

Play in our internal ladder, USCF-rated events, and the Ivy League collegiate circuit. We travel to intercollegiate tournaments each year.

♝

Community First

Blitz nights, puzzle battles, and pizza. The board is the excuse — the people are the point.

Our Mission

Growing the game on campus

The Princeton Chess Club exists to make chess accessible, social, and competitive for everyone on campus. We host weekly meetings, run free lessons for beginners, organize tournaments, and represent Princeton at collegiate events across the Northeast.

Whether you want to learn the rules, sharpen your endgame, or earn a rating, there's a seat for you at the board.
