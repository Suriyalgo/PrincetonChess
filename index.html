Who We Are

Our mission

We believe chess belongs to everyone. Our club exists to give every Princeton student — beginner or master — a welcoming place to play, learn, and belong. We meet every Friday to push pawns, trade blitz games, teach newcomers, and represent Princeton at tournaments across the country.

♙

Everyone's welcome

Never moved a piece? Perfect. We'll teach you, and someone will happily lose to you while you learn.

♞

We compete

We send teams to the Pan-Ams, the Ivy League Challenge, and rated congresses up and down the East Coast.

♛

It's about the people

The chess is the excuse. The friendships, rivalries, and late-night post-mortems — that's the club.

This organization is open to all Princeton University students interested in supporting our mission, regardless of identity, such as race, sex, ethnicity, national origin, or other protected characteristics.
